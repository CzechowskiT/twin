# TWIN Product Narrative — 2026-05-28

## Core narrative

TWIN is being built as a candidate-side career intelligence and trust layer that converts noisy job-market activity into acceptance-ready opportunities. The product goal is not inbox volume; it is a shortlist of moments worth acting on.

## Problem framing

Most products optimize for activity volume (more applications, more messages, more recruiter noise). That creates fatigue for candidates and filtering burden for employers. TWIN aims to reduce this noise by combining matching, evidence, consent, and verification signals before high-friction actions.

## Product direction pillars

1. Career Intelligence Layer
   - Contextual profile intelligence, role fit interpretation, and decision support.
2. Verified Candidate Gateway
   - Machine-assisted, auditable verification path with exception-based escalation.
3. Candidate Career Brief
   - A compact, evidence-backed representation of candidate readiness and signal quality.
4. Candidate 360
   - Unified candidate profile view across skills, trajectory, outcomes, and intent.
5. Skill Evidence Layer
   - Proof-oriented skill representation (projects, outcomes, validated artifacts).
6. Personalized Pre-Apply Feedback
   - Candidate-side quality improvement before high-stakes submissions.
7. Living Career Fit Ranking
   - Dynamic ranking based on evolving evidence, constraints, and market context.

## What TWIN is not

- Not a blind spam auto-apply bot.
- Not a guaranteed-interview machine.
- Not a claim of mass public launch before operational gates are green.

## Trust model (candidate and employer)

- Candidate trust:
  - consent-first actions
  - transparent state and preference controls
  - reduced low-value activity loops
- Employer trust:
  - better candidate signal quality
  - auditable verification trajectory
  - less manual screening noise

## Competition positioning

TWIN positions around verification-informed candidate representation and fit intelligence rather than pure application throughput:

- Candidate-side adaptation of verification-grade matching mechanics.
- Candidate Career Brief as portable, evidence-oriented profile layer.
- Candidate 360 and Skill Evidence Layer as longitudinal signal system.
- Personalized pre-apply feedback and living ranking as quality loops.
