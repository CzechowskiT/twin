# TWIN Product Pillars — 2026-05-28

## Pillars

1. Career Intelligence Layer
2. Verified Candidate Gateway
3. Candidate Career Brief
4. Verified Candidate 360
5. Skill Evidence Layer
6. Personalized Pre-Apply Feedback
7. Living Career Fit Ranking
8. Delegated Apply with Guardrails and Evidence Trail

## Operating interpretation

- TWIN is not a mass auto-apply bot.
- TWIN is not a generic job board clone.
- TWIN optimizes for acceptance-ready outcomes, not inbox volume.

## Pillar-to-execution mapping (MVP)

- Career Intelligence Layer: profile + match + feedback signals.
- Verified Candidate Gateway: explicit readiness and blocked reasons.
- Candidate Career Brief: candidate intent and hard boundaries.
- Verified Candidate 360: consolidated read model with trust tiers.
- Skill Evidence Layer: observable evidence over self-claims alone.
- Pre-Apply Feedback: quality improvements before external submission.
- Living Fit Ranking: dynamic ranking informed by candidate feedback.
- Delegated Apply Guardrails: explicit consent and policy-safe controls.
