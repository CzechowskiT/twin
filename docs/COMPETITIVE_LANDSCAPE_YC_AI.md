**Product name:** **TWIN** (do not confuse with YC company **Glimmer**, which does PDF search).

| Dimension | Observation |

## 2. Direct hiring / talent competitors (YC list)

| Company | Positioning (from list) | TWIN differentiation |
|---------|------------------------|----------------------|
| **Apriora** | AI interviewer — faster hires | TWIN: end-to-end **candidate** journey (match → apply → **calendar**), not interview-only point solution |
| **Parasale** | AI recruiter — find & engage candidates on autopilot | TWIN: **consent-first**, ranked pipeline, placement verification — not cold outbound spam |
| **DianaHR** | People ops automation — “give us your people ops” | TWIN: **talent marketplace + placement economics**; HRIS automation is complementary, not core |

**Investor line:** YC validates AI in hiring, but most entries are **point tools** or **employer-side automation**. TWIN’s wedge is **candidate-native autonomous agent → acceptance calendar**, with B2B economics on verified placement.
