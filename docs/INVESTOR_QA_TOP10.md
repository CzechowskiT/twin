# Investor Q&A — top 10 (aligned with founder materials)

Short answers for live meetings and data room. Numbers are **illustrative** unless cited from `/investor/metrics`. Deep dives: `TWIN_PITCH_ONE_PAGER.md`, `GLIMMER_GLOBJOB_GAP_ANALYSIS.md`, `COMPETITIVE_LANDSCAPE_YC_AI.md`.

### 1. What is TWIN in one sentence?

An autonomous career agent that curates global opportunities and drives candidates toward a **calendar of acceptance-ready interviews**, while employers get pre-qualified pipelines instead of CV dumps.

### 2. How are you different from LinkedIn or Indeed?

They optimize **volume and recruiter subscriptions**. TWIN optimizes **ranked fit + consent + calendar outcomes** — fewer, better moments. We do not position as “more job ads.”

### 3. How are you different from YC hiring tools (Apriora, Parasale)?

Those are **point solutions** (AI interview, outbound AI recruiter). TWIN owns the **candidate loop**: discovery → match lanes → apply → interview calendar → **placement verification**.

### 4. Are you the same as YC “Glimmer”?

**No.** YC Glimmer searches PDFs. Founder strategy used the name Glimmer for talent marketplace vision; **shipped brand is TWIN** to avoid collision.

### 5. How do you make money?

**Hybrid:** B2C subscriptions (Stripe Premium/Pro today; founder ladder adds Standby/Standard later), **B2B** placement/success fees and annual enterprise programs, optional **flat rate** on vacancy spend (founder spreadsheet model in B2B calculator). See `/for-companies` and `/calculator/b2b`.

### 6. What is the flat rate model?

For high-volume employers: fee ≈ **10% of estimated vacancy posting spend** (example: 150 roles × 500 PLN = 75k PLN traditional → 7.5k PLN program fee). Complements per-hire % models — founder chooses default in contracts.

### 7. What is the MVP today vs roadmap?

**Today:** PL scrapers, matching, apply tracking, Google calendar + ICS, auto-apply on allowlist, recruiter inbox, placement events, investor demo. **Next:** Microsoft Graph calendar, freemium offer-count teaser, three-lane UI everywhere, more boards, ATS depth.

### 8. What about scraping and GDPR?

RODO-first onboarding, documented scraping compliance, preference for **employer agreements/APIs** over aggressive anonymous scrape. Placement proof uses **in-product events**, not LinkedIn DMs.

### 9. What proof will you show in 12 months?

Wishlist → activated candidates → paid conversion → **verified placements** → 2–3 B2B logos on flat rate or success fee → calendar-linked interviews as leading indicator.

### 10. What do you need from this round?

Founder sets amount (materials cite ~**350k–1M USD** pre-seed/seed range). Use of funds: corpus + compliance, billing live, calendar integrations, first enterprise pilots, repeatable demo → production metrics.
