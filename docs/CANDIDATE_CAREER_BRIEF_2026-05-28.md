# Candidate Career Brief — 2026-05-28

## Objective

Candidate Career Brief is TWIN's structured representation of candidate intent, constraints, and representation boundaries.
It is the core artifact used to prepare high-quality applications without turning TWIN into a spam engine.

## Required sections

### Who I am

- Short professional identity statement.
- Seniority and core domain summary.

### What I want

- Role families and preferred responsibilities.
- Target company context and work style.

### What I do not want

- Hard exclusions on role type, scope, or culture.
- Non-negotiable exclusions.

### Ideal roles

- Ranked role archetypes with rationale.

### Risky roles

- Roles where fit is uncertain, requiring extra evidence or manual review.

### Strengths

- Verified and candidate-declared strengths with confidence labels.

### Salary

- Expected range and flexibility boundaries.

### Location / remote

- Accepted locations, relocation stance, and remote/hybrid constraints.

### Authorized representation boundaries

- What TWIN may do in candidate's name.
- Which channels are allowed.

### Do-not-apply rules

- Explicit company, role, geography, compensation, and channel exclusions.

### What TWIN may say

- Candidate-approved claims and positioning language.

### What TWIN must never say

- Unverified experience claims.
- Fabricated certifications, references, or outcomes.
- Any representation outside explicit candidate authorization.

## Operating rule

When brief data is incomplete, gateway status must degrade to `career_brief_missing` and delegated submit must remain blocked.
