# TWIN — Privacy Policy (United Arab Emirates)

**Version:** MVP · **Last updated:** May 2026

---

**Not legal advice.** This text is for transparency only. **Mandatory laws of the UAE** (including employment, consumer, and data-protection rules) **prevail** over any conflicting wording.

---

## Scope

If we estimate you are accessing the Service from the **United Arab Emirates**, this document describes processing with **UAE Federal Decree-Law No. 45 of 2021 on the Protection of Personal Data** (commonly referred to as the **UAE PDPL**) in mind. It does **not** replace legal counsel or sector-specific obligations that may apply to you.

---

## What we collect

- Account **email** and **password** (stored hashed).
- **Career profile** fields you choose to provide (skills, experience, salary expectations, approximate location).
- **Application and match history** within TWIN.
- If you upload a **CV** or **voice intro**: file contents and **derived signals** (e.g. skills, summaries) used to operate the product, including automated parsing where enabled.
- **Approximate country / region** from IP and CDN headers, or from **browser geolocation** if you consent, to show **jurisdiction-aware** notices. We do **not** use this for continuous tracking.

---

## Lawful bases

Where required we rely on **consent**; we may also rely on **performance of a contract** or other lawful grounds recognised under the **UAE PDPL** when applicable.

---

## Your rights

Depending on circumstances you may exercise rights such as **access**, **rectification**, **erasure**, **restriction**, **objection**, or **portability** as provided by applicable law. Contact the TWIN operator through the channel published on the site.

---

## Retention

We keep data while your account is active and the purpose requires it, and delete or anonymise it when no longer needed unless a **longer period** is required by law.

---

## International transfers & subprocessors

We use **Anthropic Claude** for matching and drafting assistance. Data may be processed outside the UAE; transfers should be covered by **appropriate safeguards** where the PDPL requires them (e.g. contractual clauses or other approved mechanisms).

---

## Cookies & local storage {#cookies}

We use **strictly necessary** storage for authentication, security, and language. If you accept **optional** analytics or marketing cookies, we may enable those tags when the features ship. Your choice is stored on this device and can be changed from the **site footer**.

Use the contact method shown on the website for privacy requests.
